Problem statement
Build a multi-agent energy system for a housing society. Every home has an energy
agent that knows its residents’ preferences, such as “EV charged by 8 am” or “bedroom
at 24°C by 10 pm.” A society agent coordinates the homes. A grid agent sends price and
demand-response signals. The agents negotiate and trade surplus solar peer to peer,
reschedule flexible loads, and respond to grid stress events, while every resident’s 
constraints are respected.

Why this matters
Rooftop solar, home batteries, EVs and smart ACs are spreading quickly through Indian
housing societies. The grid sees them as uncontrolled load. On a hot evening, thousands of
ACs and EV chargers spike demand at the same moment, while daytime solar is wasted or
sold back cheaply. Real coordination would need every device to negotiate with every other
device and with the grid.
